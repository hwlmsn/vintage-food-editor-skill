# Background Palette Catalog

Use these as solid tabletop or seamless background directions. Preserve believable shadows and food/background separation.

| No. | Palette | Anchor | Character | Best with | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 番茄红 Tomato Red | `#B93A2F` | Warm, bold, classic food advertising | grilled meat, fried food, noodles, beige ceramics | Do not let red sauces merge into the background |
| 2 | 酒红 Burgundy | `#7D2630` | Deep, mature, restrained luxury | steak, dark sauces, desserts, glassware | Lift shadows so dark food stays readable |
| 3 | 奶油黄 Butter Yellow | `#E3BB61` | Bright, friendly, nostalgic | breakfast, bread, coffee, brown food | Keep pale food edges distinct |
| 4 | 芥末黄 Mustard | `#B58A2A` | Earthy mid-century print character | barbecue, skewers, pastries, blue-white plates | Avoid dirty green-yellow casts on food |
| 5 | 墨绿 Forest Green | `#254638` | Calm, premium, cinematic | red meat, seafood, white ceramics | Keep greens in garnish from disappearing |
| 6 | 橄榄绿 Olive | `#6E713D` | Soft retro dining-room mood | pasta, bread, roasted vegetables | Protect natural vegetable color separation |
| 7 | 焦糖橙 Burnt Orange | `#C56332` | Warm, lively, 1970s commercial feel | grilled food, cream dishes, dark tableware | Avoid oversaturating fried surfaces |
| 8 | 可可棕 Cocoa Brown | `#4C332B` | Rich, tactile, old-magazine warmth | cakes, coffee, pale dishes, silverware | Raise tonal contrast for brown foods |
| 9 | 棕黑 Espresso Black | `#24201E` | Dramatic, minimal, premium | bright dishes, glossy sauces, cocktails | Keep blacks open; never crush food detail |
| 10 | 孔雀蓝 Teal Blue | `#225B61` | Cool contrast, distinctive retro editorial | orange-red food, seafood, warm ceramics | Avoid cyan contamination on highlights |

## Recommendation rules

- Favor complementary contrast over matching the dominant food color.
- For a coherent restaurant set, first try one palette across the batch.
- If the dish is predominantly red or orange, prefer 3, 5, 9, or 10.
- If the dish is predominantly beige, brown, or fried, prefer 1, 5, 7, or 10.
- If the dish is pale, creamy, or white, prefer 2, 5, 8, or 9.
- If there is already strong green garnish, prefer 1, 2, 3, or 7 rather than green backgrounds.
- Dark plates need a light or mid-tone background; pale plates can tolerate dark palettes.
